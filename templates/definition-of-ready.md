# Definition of ready

A story is ready to write when the input answers all five:

- [ ] Who's affected: the people or teams who feel the problem.
- [ ] The problem: what goes wrong today, in their words.
- [ ] The desired outcome: what is true when it's fixed.
- [ ] Which system or application: where the change happens.
- [ ] Known constraints: deadlines, rules, limits or things that can't change.

If any are missing, write no story. Reply with "# Not ready yet", name what is missing, and ask at most five specific questions.
