Subscriptions project, accepted July 2026

Let subscribers change what's in their box from their account

As a subscriber, I want to change what's in my next box from my account, so that my next box holds what I want.

Acceptance criteria
- Given my box ships in more than 72 hours, when I change what's in it, then the change is saved.
- Given my box is inside the cutoff, when I try to change it, then the change is blocked.
- Given I changed my box, when an agent opens me in Stockroom, then they see the change within a minute.

Estimate: for a developer who knows the web app.
