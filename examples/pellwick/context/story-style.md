title: Story style
updated: 2026-09-14
sources:
  - Notes "How we write tickets" by Priya Raman, product manager, 2026-09-11, based on ten accepted Jira stories from July and August 2026
---
- User stories for anything a subscriber sees. Job stories for staff tools. Bugs use the bug template. ("How we write tickets", Priya Raman, 2026-09-11)
- Change requests, when another team asks to change a rule or setting, use the team template in templates/change-request.md. ("How we write tickets", Priya Raman, 2026-09-11)
- Titles start with a verb and name who benefits, for example "Let subscribers skip a box from their account". ("How we write tickets", Priya Raman, 2026-09-11)
- Acceptance criteria cover the cutoff and what support sees in Stockroom, since those are the parts that get missed. ("How we write tickets", Priya Raman, 2026-09-11)
- Estimates assume a developer who knows the web app. Add 50% when Stockroom changes too. ("How we write tickets", Priya Raman, 2026-09-11)
- When a ticket quotes Helpline reason counts, it gives the month they are from. ("How we write tickets", Priya Raman, 2026-09-11)
