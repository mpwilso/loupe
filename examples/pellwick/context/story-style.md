---
title: Story style
updated: 2026-09-14
sources:
  - Ten accepted Jira stories from July and August 2026
  - Interview with Priya Raman, product manager, 2026-09-11
---
- User stories for anything a subscriber sees. Job stories for staff tools. Bugs use the bug template.
- Titles start with a verb and name who benefits, for example "Let subscribers skip a box from their account".
- Acceptance criteria cover the cutoff and what support sees in Stockroom, since those are the parts that get missed.
- Estimates assume a developer who knows the web app. Add 50% when Stockroom changes too.
- Numbers from Helpline reason counts go in Known, with the month.
