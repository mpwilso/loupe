In order, for October to December 2026:

1. Cut support contacts. Helpline handled 4,100 tickets in August. The goal is 3,000 a month by December. Anything that lets customers help themselves ranks high.
2. Keep subscribers. Cancellations run at 3.1% a month. The goal is under 2.5%.
3. Holiday readiness. No risky changes to checkout or payments from 1 December to 5 January.

## Known tech debt
- The delivery schedule logic lives in both the web app and Stockroom. They sometimes disagree.
- Payment status updates arrive late, up to 10 minutes after a charge.
