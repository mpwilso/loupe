## Trial 8b: the fixes, rerun

After trial 8, the checker was changed to fail a tracker key anywhere in a story unless its line ends with a valid live source for that key. The tracker steps were told to cite at least one fact from each relevant issue as a Known line and to leave out issues that aren't relevant. They were also told to say nothing of a tracker, Jira or issue search when no tracker is loaded, and to answer a request to change the tracker with exactly one line. The duplicate question now ends with its live source, since the checker needs one on every line that names a key.

Steps A, D and E were rerun the same way, in a fresh copy of trial 8's starting repository. Step B was run again first, only to give D its conversation. It cost about $0.89. These results are from the saved outputs in `build/trial-8b/`, for the advisor to score.
