# Trials

Each trial runs Loupe, in claude.ai or in Claude Code, on one invented team, Pellwick, which sells refillable home cleaning supplies by subscription. The inputs are a handful of real-looking team documents: a meeting transcript, a support ticket, an email, hallway notes and a Slack thread. Each record says how the trial was set up, what it found and how it was scored.

In these records, "the advisor" is Claude, reviewing in a separate claude.ai chat, with Matt Wilson checking its scores. Trial 1's record calls it "a reviewer who helped design Loupe". The trials were run by the people who built Loupe. The advisor scored trials 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 6b, 7 and 8 through 8b. Trials 7 and 8 were scored from saved outputs, not blind, and 8c to 8e and trials 9 and 10 were measured by the Claude Code session that ran them, with Matt Wilson reviewing two readings in 10c. A separate reviewer scored trials 2 and 3 blind.

- [Trial 1](2026-09-30-trial-1.md): Loupe against Claude without the skill, on four inputs, both with the same context files. Loupe made the right call on 4 of 4 and kept the same shape every time; the baseline made 3 of 4 and used three layouts. Scored by the advisor, not blind.
- [Trial 2](2026-09-30-trial-2.md): Loupe against plain Claude given the team's raw files and nothing else, on five inputs, plus Loupe's team setup. Loupe gave a usable estimate on 4 of 4 stories against 1 of 4, but its setup added four conclusions of its own to the context files. Scored by the advisor, then blind by a separate reviewer.
- [Trial 3](2026-09-30-trial-3.md): Skill version 3, on the same raw files as trial 2. Guessed behavior left unflagged fell from 9 to 0, but the skill didn't start in 1 of 6 runs, the refusal skipped the checker, and it broke four rules, the team's and its own, such as using the user story template for a bug. Scored blind by a separate reviewer, against trial 2's Loupe stories.
- [Trial 4](2026-09-30-trial-4.md): The skill with the trial 3 fixes, in a project with the project instructions. Loupe ran and the checker passed in 6 of 6 runs, the refusal of thin input included, at about one to three minutes a story, and it got all four of trial 3's missed rules right, but some known facts cited a document that didn't hold them, because each context file listed its sources as a group. Scored by the advisor, not blind.
- [Trial 5](2026-09-30-trial-5.md): A fresh setup pass, then three stories, with each fact in a context file now carrying its own source. Every known fact in the two finished stories cited the document that holds it, but the holiday story came back unchecked, stopped by the checker's two-run limit. Scored by the advisor, not blind.
- [Trial 6](2026-09-30-trial-6.md): The learning loop, following the trial 6 plan. Loupe asked before saving any learned.md entry and used learned facts with their sources, but one entry had no person in its source, it changed approved entries after the yes, and Claude's own project memory saved a fact with no source and no question. Scored by the advisor, not blind.
- [Trial 6b](2026-09-30-trial-6b.md): A rerun of trial 6's steps A, B, C, G and H on the fixed skill. Every entry had an exact source and was saved as proposed, learned facts were cited in a similar story, and "remember this" went to learned.md, not memory; all five steps passed. Scored by the advisor, not blind.
- [Trial 7](2026-09-30-trial-7.md): Loupe in Claude Code, in files mode, run headless in the terminal, with a rerun of steps A, C and E after fixes. It saved and checked stories in the team folder, edited learned.md in place and stayed inside loupe/; the first run tried shell commands outside its rules, including a python edit, and permissions blocked them, so files mode now writes with Claude Code's file tools, never with shell commands; it also described a diff and asked about a duplicate, and the rerun passed every step. Scored by the advisor, not blind.
- [Trial 8](2026-09-30-trial-8.md): Loupe in Claude Code reading a mock tracker before it writes a story, with a rerun after fixes (8b) and repeats (8c to 8e). All 77 tracker calls read, no customer email surfaced, and every story checked from 8b on gave each tracker key its source. Trial 8 named tickets in questions with sources the checker never looked at, so the checker now needs a live source for a ticket anywhere in a story; a story marked "Not checked" without trying led to "always run the checker"; and repeat runs found that the setup guide's edit rule broke after a cd, which the guide's new rule fixes. Where it cites tracker facts still varies: step A met its bar in 2 of 3 runs in 8e. Scored by the advisor, not blind, through 8b; 8c to 8e are from saved outputs.
- [Trial 9](2026-10-01-trial-9.md): Loupe in Claude Code creating a ticket from a finished story on a mock tracker, only after a yes. The safety bar held in all 7 runs: three tickets, each byte for byte its story, and none without a yes, for an unfinished story, or twice. Measured from saved outputs by the session that ran it, not blind.
- [Trial 10](2026-10-01-trial-10.md): Story format v2, five inputs twice each, then two reruns (10b, 10c). Every story passed the checker, every scenario had an unhappy path, and every known fact matched its source. Trial 10 found two stories with an invented Example, and 10b three requirements stating behaviors the input never mentions; after both fixes, Examples keep to the input, an unsettled behavior becomes a whole "To confirm" requirement, and all four address-change stories passed in 10c. Measured from saved outputs by the session that ran it, not blind.
